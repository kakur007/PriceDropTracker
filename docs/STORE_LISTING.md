## Full description

Want it, but not at today's price? Price Genius keeps an eye on it for you.

Open a product in almost any online store, click the Price Genius icon and press Track price. We check the price in the background and let you know when it drops. No account, no copying links, no spreadsheet.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Open a product page in any online store.
2. Click the Price Genius icon (or press Alt+Shift+T) and choose Track price.
3. Get a notification when the price drops, or when a sold-out product is back in stock.

Price not found on some shop? Click "Pick price on page" and point at it. Price Genius remembers that spot and checks it from then on.

IS THIS DEAL REAL?
"−50%!" isn't always what it seems. On products you track, Price Genius compares the discount with the prices it actually saw, and tells you when a deal is inflated or when it was cheaper a few days ago. If the store shows its lowest price of the last 30 days, that's checked too.

ALERTS THAT FIT EACH PRODUCT
• Any drop, a drop of at least X %, or below a price you choose
• Back-in-stock alerts
• Sizes, pack sizes and colours are tracked separately, each with its own price
• A daily limit, so you're never flooded with notifications

WORKS ON ALMOST ANY STORE
Big marketplaces, fashion and electronics stores, and small independent shops alike. Prices in 30+ currencies, shown in each store's own currency.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no sign-up
• Your products and price history are stored only in your browser
• No ads, no analytics, nothing sold
• Background checks run without your store logins, so they can never touch your cart or account

ALSO INCLUDED
• A small price card on product pages (can be switched off)
• Price history chart for every product
• Search, sort and filter your list
• Export to CSV for Excel or Google Sheets
• Light and dark mode
